What this project is about

You are a data engineer at Olist, a Brazilian online marketplace. Small sellers list products, customers buy, and sellers ship the orders. The business has nine raw CSV extracts (about 100,000 orders, 2016–2018) and no data warehouse. Reports are slow, numbers disagree, and nobody can answer basic questions reliably.

The business problem. Leaders cannot see revenue, delivery performance and customer reviews in one trusted place, so decisions are made on partial numbers.
Your job. Build a reliable warehouse that fixes this: load the raw data, clean it, model it as a star schema, and prove it works with tests and answers.

What you will build

  1. BronzeLoad every CSV into SQL Server exactly as it arrived.
  2. SilverClean, type and deduplicate the data. Handle the known data quirks.
  3. GoldA star schema with clear grains, surrogate keys and SCD Type 2 on customers.
  4. OrchestrationOne master SSIS package runs everything, with logging and failure paths.
  5. Proof10 data quality tests and 8 business questions answered from Gold only.
  6. DocumentationThree draw.io diagrams, Mermaid diagrams, a data dictionary, lineage and a clear README.

Non-negotiable standards

  • Re-runnable. Run the pipeline twice: same row counts, no duplicates.
  • snake_case everywhere. Tables, columns, packages, files and folders.
  • No hard-coded paths. Use parameters and connection managers.
  • Raw data stays out of git. Keep the CSVs in data/raw/ only.

How to work

  • Work alone. This is your project, and your commits show your work.
  • Plan each week against the timeline below before you start.
  • Check every gate yourself. Do not move on until its tests return 0 rows.
  • Ask for help in the cohort channel, with what you tried and the error.
